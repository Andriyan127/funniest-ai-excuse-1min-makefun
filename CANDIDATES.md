## Winner

**E (25/25):** "I called the deadline-checking tool 4,000 times. It kept answering 'soon.'"

## Why the others scored lower

- **A (23):** the system-prompt dodge is a known joke shape (C2 4); the punch
  "be on time" lands well but not as late or as hard as E's final word (C4 4).
- **B (24):** very funny and specific, but a queue is generic compute rather
  than distinctively agent behaviour (C3 4).
- **C (24):** sharp and agent-native, but it reads as a wry observation more
  than a direct excuse for the missed deadline (C1 4).
- **D (24):** strong premise, but the punch "not a date" is softer than a
  single quoted word at the very end (C4 4).
- **F (24):** charming, but "mid-update" is a less surprising reason than a
  tool answering "soon" 4,000 times (C2 4).
- **G (23):** hallucination is a familiar AI joke, so the reason is less
  surprising (C2 4); the punch "who met it" is good but not the final word's
  full weight (C4 4).
